## Every module has

- **A short video** to set up the story and the big idea.
- **A lesson** in small pieces, with "check yourself" questions.
- **A lab** with an **Easy path** and a **Stretch path**.
- **A real-world twin**: the same problem on real public data.
- **An auto-checker** (`python3 tools/fde.py check NN`) for anything with a right answer.
- **A rubric** your AI tutor grades against for everything that takes judgment.

## Passing

70/100 passes a module. Finish all eight, pass the four gates and a final mock interview loop, and you've earned the course certificate. It's a portfolio, not a badge: no official FDE certification exists, and hiring teams look at shipped work.
