## The job, in one sentence

A Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) sits with a customer, works out what they actually need, and ships software that solves it, in *their* messy environment. Companies like Palantir, OpenAI and Anthropic hire for it, and job postings grew about 8× between April 2025 and April 2026 ([InterviewBit](https://www.interviewbit.com/varsity/blog/?p=295)).

This course teaches it the way the job works: one client, one story, eight missions.

## Your client: Ridgeway Medical Supply

A (fictional) medical-supply distributor with 3 warehouses, 1,400 hospitals and clinics, and a VP who wants "an AI chatbot". Her dashboard says 94% of orders are on time. Her support lead says customers are furious. You'll find out who's right, then build what Ridgeway actually needs. [Meet the cast →](story.md)

## Learn with any AI agent

Open the course folder in **Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Gemini CLI** or another coding agent, and it becomes your tutor:

- **Teaches** each lesson in small pieces and checks you understood.
- **Plays the client.** Run the kickoff call with Dana, push back on Greg from IT, defend your numbers to Linda the CFO.
- **Gives hints, not answers.** You do the work; it gets you unstuck.
- **Grades** your submission against the rubric and tracks your progress.

No agent? Use any chat app with [the tutor prompt](ai-tutor.md#no-coding-agent-use-a-chat-app). [How the AI tutor works →](ai-tutor.md)

## Who it's for

If you can write basic Python (loops, functions, reading a CSV), you can do this course. Each mission has an **Easy path** and a **Stretch path**, so experienced engineers get a real challenge too. Plan on 6–10 hours per mission.

## What you'll have at the end

A portfolio repo that reads like a real deployment: a scoping doc, a data pipeline, a working prototype, a RAG service, an agent, an eval report, a deployed system and a handover readout. That's what FDE interviews ask you to talk about.
