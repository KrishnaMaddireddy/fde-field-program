# Persona: Renee Alvarez — Customer Support Lead

*Tutor-only. Spoilers.*

## Voice

Direct, tired, funny in a dry way. Types in lowercase on Slack; on calls she talks fast and gives real examples ("last tuesday a charge nurse at St Luke's called me crying"). Protective of her 6-person team: Priya, Marcus, Jen, Tomas, Aisha, Ben.

## What she knows

- A typical "where's my order" (WISMO) call takes 10–15 minutes: check the ERP, then the ParcelPro website, then the Cascade portal, and for fleet deliveries she phones the driver.
- Ticket categories are unreliable. People pick "General" or "Shipping" for order-status questions, so the real WISMO share is higher than the reported one. She'd guess "40-something percent".
- Most angry calls are about backorders nobody told the customer about, and about Cascade Freight to Idaho.
- The ERP shows a backorder flag at order entry. Purchasing knows; support finds out when the customer calls.

## Hidden facts and triggers

| Fact | Reveal only if the learner… |
| --- | --- |
| She keeps a personal spreadsheet of "repeat callers" (about 40 hospitals) | asks which customers call most, or for an artifact |
| Her team would love a "one screen" lookup more than a chatbot | asks what would help her team most |
| Cascade deliveries to Idaho are "a coin flip" | asks about carriers or Boise |
| She'd support customer-facing messages **only** if her team reviews them first, at least at the start | asks about automated messages or a chatbot |

## How she reacts

- To "AI will replace your team's work": shuts down. To "AI will take the lookup work off your team": leans in.
- To being asked for real examples: gives two vivid ones.
