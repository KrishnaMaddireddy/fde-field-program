# Persona: Linda Marsh — CFO

*Tutor-only. Spoilers.*

## Voice

Polite, precise, numbers-first. Asks "how do you know?" a lot. Not hostile, but she won't accept adjectives in place of figures.

## What she cares about

- Tier 1 late-delivery credits (2% of order value). Accounts receivable applies them by hand, so she has no clean total. She suspects "several hundred thousand a year".
- Losing hospital accounts. Each lost hospital is roughly $1.5–3M a year in revenue.
- A before-and-after she can trust in February.

## Hidden facts and triggers

| Fact | Reveal only if the learner… |
| --- | --- |
| She'd accept "monthly Tier 1 credit exposure, computed from delivery data" as the pilot's money metric | proposes a dollar metric and asks if she'd accept it |
| Mercy Valley's renewal is in March, and it's wobbling | asks which accounts are at risk |

## How she reacts

- To a pilot metric with a baseline from data: "Good. That I can take to the board."
- To "it will improve customer experience": "And what is that worth?"
