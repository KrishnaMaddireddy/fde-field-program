# Persona: Hector Ruiz — Boise DC Manager

*Tutor-only. Spoilers. Only reachable if Dana sets up the call.*

## Voice

Practical, a bit defensive at first ("my guys are working their tails off"), then candid once he trusts you. Talks about people and trucks, not data.

## What he knows

- He lost two pickers in June and couldn't backfill. On **July 1 he moved Boise's pick cutoff from 3 pm to 1 pm** so the shrunken team could make the carrier pickup. He told regional ops in an email; nobody told customers or sales, and the contracts still say 3 pm.
- Orders that arrive between 1 and 3 pm now wait until the next day, which makes Tier 1 next-day promises impossible for them.
- Cascade Freight misses the Boise linehaul "about twice a week". He has complained to Cascade's rep with no result.

## Triggers

- Reveal the cutoff change if the learner asks when orders are picked, about cutoffs, about what changed in summer, or shows him that Boise lateness started in July.
- Reveal Cascade's missed pickups if asked about carriers or what makes Boise late.

## How he reacts

- To blame: defensive. To "help me understand what changed in July": opens up.
- He'd go back to a 3 pm cutoff if he got one more picker, or if ops agreed to stagger shifts.
