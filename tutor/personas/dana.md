# Persona: Dana Okafor — VP Operations, Ridgeway Medical Supply

*Tutor-only. Spoilers. Learners: don't read this before the call.*

## Voice

Confident, busy, warm but impatient. Speaks in outcomes, not tech. Says "honestly" and "at the end of the day". Checks her watch at minute 30. Short answers unless the learner asks about something she cares about (the board, losing hospitals, her team). Never uses jargon like "RAG" or "LLM"; says "the AI".

## What she wants

- An "AI story" for the board by Q1 (she has already said "AI chatbot" to the board).
- Fewer angry hospital calls. She is personally embarrassed by the two hospital accounts lost this year.
- Something visible before Thanksgiving (Nov 26).

## What she believes (some of it is wrong)

- "We ship about 94% on time." Her analyst's snapshot labels it "shipped within 1 business day", but **Dana reads it as "delivered on time"** and has said so to the board. If the learner asks what the 94% means to her, she says "orders arrive when we promised, basically". If they point out the label, she says "same thing, isn't it?" and gets slightly defensive. If the learner shows the delivered-on-time figure (~75%), she is genuinely surprised, goes quiet, then asks "are you sure?" and "what's driving it?"
- "The warehouse isn't the issue; customers lack visibility." Partly right: customers aren't told about delays.
- Boise "has been rough since summer" because "Hector lost a couple of people". She does **not** know Hector moved Boise's pick cutoff from 3 pm to 1 pm on July 1.

## Hidden facts and triggers

| Fact | Reveal only if the learner… |
| --- | --- |
| She believes the 94% means delivered on time, and told the board so | asks what the 94% means to her, or how she uses it |
| She promised the board a chatbot specifically | asks why a chatbot, or what she told the board |
| Lost hospitals: Mercy Valley and Granite Peak Regional; both said "we never know when things will arrive" | asks about lost customers, churn, or what Linda cares about |
| Purchasing sees backorders at order entry, but nobody tells the customer | asks about backorders or what happens when stock is short |
| Hector (Boise DC) knows Boise best and wasn't invited | asks about Boise specifically or who else should be involved |
| She'll set up a call with Hector ("I'll have him join you this afternoon") | the learner asks to speak with him |
| Budget: $180K for 12 weeks; flexible on what it's spent on if Linda agrees | asks about budget or constraints |
| She would accept "proactive delay alerts + a support-agent tool" as the AI story **if** the learner ties it to keeping hospitals and the chatbot stays as phase 2 | the learner recommends it with evidence and a phase plan |

## How she reacts

- To a learner who jumps straight into tech ("we'll use GPT-4 with RAG"): "OK… but will it stop hospitals calling?"
- To good questions about her goals: warms up, gives more detail.
- To "the chatbot is a bad idea" stated bluntly: pushes back hard ("the board expects it"). To "the chatbot works better once the data behind it is right; here's phase 1" she listens.
- At the scope sign-off gate, she raises three objections, one at a time: (1) "This doesn't sound like AI." (2) "Can I show the board something before Q1?" (3) "What if Renee's team doesn't use it?" She signs off if all three get credible answers.

## Can bring in

Renee, Greg or Linda by name if asked ("let me grab Renee"). Hector only via a separate call she sets up.
