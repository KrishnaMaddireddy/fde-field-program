# Walkthrough video script — Module 1 (record this yourself)

**Format:** screen recording with your voice, 8–10 minutes, 1280×720 or 1920×1080. Optional face cam in a corner.
**Goal:** show a learner what *doing* Module 1 with an AI tutor looks like, without giving away the answers.
**Rule:** never show final numbers, the solutions folder, or the persona files on screen.

| Time | On screen | Say (roughly, in your own words) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | You, or the course homepage | "Hi, I'm Krish. In this video I'll show you how to work through Mission 1 with an AI agent as your tutor. You'll see me get stuck, too." |
| 0:30 | Terminal: `python3 tools/fde.py doctor` | "First, the setup check. It builds Ridgeway's data, which is identical for everyone, so your answers can be auto-checked." |
| 1:00 | Agent: type `Start the course` | "Any agent works. I'm using ___. It reads AGENTS.md and becomes my tutor." Show it asking about your background. |
| 2:00 | `modules/01-discovery/inbox/` | Read Dana's email and Renee's Slack aloud. "Already they disagree. That's our first clue." |
| 3:00 | Notebook or editor: load the ERP files | "The September files have different column names. This is real client life." Show the error, then the fix. |
| 4:30 | Run `python3 tools/fde.py check 01` with one wrong answer | "It says I'm too low, with a hint. It never shows the answer." Ask the tutor "I'm stuck"; show the first hint only. |
| 5:30 | Agent: `Start the kickoff call with Dana` | Ask two or three questions. Show Dana answering in character. Stop before anything important is revealed: "I'll let you find the rest." |
| 7:00 | `templates/scoping_doc.md` | "Two pages max. The hardest part is the out-of-scope list." Show the headings only. |
| 8:00 | Agent: `Grade Module 1` on an earlier draft | Show one piece of rubric feedback. "Honest feedback beats a nice score." |
| 8:45 | Course site, Mission 1 page | "Watch the 3-minute explainer, then dive in. See you in Mission 2." |

**Recording tips:** increase your terminal and editor font size to at least 18 pt; hide notifications; record in short takes and join them; keep each take under two minutes.
