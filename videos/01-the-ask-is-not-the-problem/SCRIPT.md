# Narration script — Module 1: The ask is not the problem

Read these lines as a voice-over. Timings match the rendered video.

- **00:00** Mission 1. Before you build anything, find out what's really going on.
- **00:05** Meet Dana, VP of Operations at Ridgeway Medical Supply. Your new client.
- **00:10** She knows what she wants: an AI chatbot. And she says 94% of orders are on time.
- **00:17** Then Renee, who runs support, messages you. She says customers are furious about late orders.
- **00:24** Both can't be fully right. Your first job is to find out which, before writing a single line of code.
- **00:30** Lesson one: the ask is not the problem. A chatbot is a solution Dana imagined.
- **00:36** Ask: if this worked perfectly, what would be different? Fewer calls.
- **00:42** Why do customers call? Because orders are late, and nobody warned them.
- **00:48** And what does that cost? Money, and hospitals walking away. That's the real problem.
- **00:56** An FDE wears three hats. The engineer asks: can we actually build this, here?
- **01:01** The consultant asks: is this the right problem, and who has to say yes?
- **01:07** And the product scout asks: what should we take back to our own product?
- **01:13** Next, map the people. Who can fund or stop this? Whose day does it change?
- **01:19** And notice who's missing. Boise has been rough since summer, and nobody invited its manager.
- **01:26** Now the trap. Dana's 94% measures when orders leave the warehouse.
- **01:31** Customers feel when orders arrive. That number lives in the carrier data, and you'll calculate it.
- **01:38** Always ask how a number is calculated. Real data has the same trap; you'll find it in the lab.
- **01:45** Then decompose. Step one: clarify the goal, and what success looks like.
- **01:51** Two: who wins, who loses, who decides, and how it's measured today.
- **01:56** Three: the inputs. What data exists, who owns it, and how messy it is.
- **02:02** Four: break it into pieces, and tackle the riskiest assumption first.
- **02:07** Five: the walking skeleton. The thinnest version that proves value in days.
- **02:13** The classic mistake, in interviews and on the job: jumping to step five first.
- **02:19** Your mission: check the numbers, interview Dana, and write a scope she'll sign. Your AI tutor is ready.
- **02:27** See you on the kickoff call.
