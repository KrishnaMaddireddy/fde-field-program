# Interview track

About 2 hours a week alongside the modules. Keep your work in this folder.

## Weekly

- [ ] One timed decomposition drill (say `drill` in chat; I invent a new client each time)
- [ ] One practical coding exercise (from Week 2) or one STAR story (from Week 4)

## Practical coding set

Do each in under 45 minutes, with tests. Save as `coding/<name>.py`.

1. Token-bucket rate limiter, thread-safe
2. HTTP client with retry, exponential backoff and jitter; respects `Retry-After`
3. Messy CSV parser: mixed date formats, thousands separators, stray whitespace, duplicate rows
4. Streaming JSON-lines reader that survives malformed lines and reports them
5. In-memory job queue with workers, retries and a dead-letter list
6. Mini RAG: chunk 5 documents, retrieve top-k with BM25, answer with citations

## Story bank

Fill `why_fde.md` (60 seconds, spoken) in Week 1 and `star_stories.md` by Week 6.
