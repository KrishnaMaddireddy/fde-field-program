# STAR stories (60–90 seconds each)

For each: **Situation** (2 sentences) · **Task** (your job, 1 sentence) · **Action** (what *you* did, 3–4 sentences, with "I") · **Result** (a number, and what you learned).

1. Ownership — you owned an outcome nobody assigned to you
2. Ambiguity — you started with a vague ask and made it concrete
3. Customer conflict — a stakeholder disagreed with you and you resolved it
4. Failure — something you shipped broke; what you did after
5. Scope cut — you shipped less, on purpose, and it was the right call
6. Technical deep dive — the hardest bug or design problem you've solved
7. Influence without authority — you changed another team's plan
8. Learning fast — you became useful in an unfamiliar domain within days
